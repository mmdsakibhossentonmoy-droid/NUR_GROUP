Screen Print Unit

Our printing unit is fully export oriented. The first and foremost policy of this factory is to satisfy our customers fully. To fulfill buyers requirements and to deliver on time, we have an experienced printing team with a strong quality department to ensure 100% excellent quality. A well-organized sampling department also works behind the scene to give a fast and accurate sample delivery within the minimum time.

We specialize in: Rubber print, Flock print, Foil print, Discharge print, Caviar print, High-density & Puff print, Glitter & Metallic print, Gelprint, Heat transfer print, Pigment print, Plastisolprint, Process print, Crack print, Devosprint, Embused Print.
