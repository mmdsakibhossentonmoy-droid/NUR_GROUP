Mission & Vision

Our Mission: To establish Nur Group as a global market leader by delivering unparalleled quality in knitted garments, bridging the best of Bangladeshi craftsmanship with the demands of the international market.

Our Vision: To pioneer sustainable growth by operating as an eco-friendly organization, deeply rooted in social responsibility and ethical manufacturing practices.

Message from Managing Director

"Since 1994, Nur Group has been defined by our core philosophy: 'Customer and Quality Above All.' Our journey from a local enterprise to a global RMG leader is a result of our unwavering commitment to excellence, innovation, and sustainable practices. We don't just manufacture garments; we build lasting trust through integrity and precision. As we look ahead, we remain dedicated to delivering international standards while fostering an environmentally responsible future."

Syed Nazmul Hassan

Managing Director, Nur Group
